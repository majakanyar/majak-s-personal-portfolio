01 Telecommunications Converting Knowledge Into Revenue
↓
What
A sales and technical support programme for a leading US telecommunications company was underperforming commercially. Agents knew the product catalogue but couldn't convert on live calls. Conversion was flat. AHT was inflated.
How
Redesigned the programme from scratch — built real call flow simulations in Articulate 360, objection-handling scenarios in Storyline tied to live CRM data, and a nesting coaching cadence that reinforced trained behaviours on the floor.
Articulate 360 Storyline Salesforce CRM Nesting Coaching Model
Result
Sales conversion +10%
Overall sales growth +13%
AHT reduced −50 seconds
Client extended scope.
The Challenge

The existing training programme for one of the largest US telecommunications accounts was delivering agents who could recite product specs but couldn't sell them. Conversion rates were stagnant. Average handle time was inflated — agents were spending extra time on calls because they lacked the conversational frameworks to guide customers to decisions efficiently.

The client was watching these numbers. The training function needed to prove it could move commercial metrics, not just produce graduates.

The Approach

I identified the core problem: the programme was knowledge-heavy and interaction-light. Agents were being assessed on what they knew, not on what they could do under pressure.

I redesigned the curriculum around simulated live call scenarios in Articulate 360 and Storyline, using real objection patterns extracted from QA data. Every scenario was tied to Salesforce CRM workflows so agents were practising the actual system they'd use on the floor. I also restructured the nesting period — introducing a daily coaching cadence that tracked and reinforced trained behaviours before they had time to decay.

The Result

Sales conversion increased 10%. Overall sales grew 13% within the quarter. AHT dropped by 50 seconds — not because agents were rushing, but because they were navigating calls with greater confidence and structure.

The client extended their scope with the operation. The redesigned programme became the standard delivery model for the account.

02 Logistics & Healthcare Training That Travels Across Industries
↓
What
Agents supporting a global logistics operator and a US healthcare provider were completing standard onboarding but underperforming on CSAT and tools usage. The training wasn't translating to floor behaviour.
How
Rebuilt both programmes around scenario-based learning derived from real QA data. Added empathy-led conversation frameworks for healthcare, system navigation simulations for logistics. Introduced a shared L&D and operations accountability model during nesting — the first of its kind on these accounts.
Rise 360 Scenario-Based Design QA Integration Nesting Coaching Model
Result
CSAT +7–10% across both accounts
Tools utilisation +25%
Nesting model adopted globally by logistics client.
The Challenge

Two very different accounts — one supporting a world-leading logistics operator, another a major US healthcare provider — shared the same problem: agents were passing training but not performing. CSAT scores were below target. Tools utilisation was poor despite the systems being covered in onboarding.

Standard onboarding was the problem. It delivered content. It didn't build behaviour.

The Approach

For both accounts I rebuilt the programmes around real customer interaction patterns sourced directly from QA data — not hypothetical scenarios, but the actual conversations agents were failing on the floor.

For healthcare, I designed empathy-led conversation frameworks given the emotional sensitivity of customer interactions. For logistics, I built exact replicas of the live system interface so agents practised navigation in a zero-risk environment before their first real call. Both programmes included a nesting coaching model that created formal accountability between L&D and operations for post-training performance — the first time these two functions shared responsibility on either account.

The Result

CSAT improved 7–10% across both accounts. Tools utilisation increased 25%. The nesting coaching model was subsequently adopted globally by the logistics client — taken beyond the account and rolled out as a standard practice across their operation.

03 Operations · Scale-Up Scaling Without Breaking
↓
What
Two of the largest US accounts needed to scale from 1,500 to 2,200 agents in 10 months. Training was already failing — attrition was high, performance declining, and the client relationship under visible strain. Scaling the same broken system was not an option.
How
Rebuilt the system before scaling it: in-class assessment activities to identify at-risk agents early, a full SOP redesign for the training-to-operations transition, and cross-functional review calls connecting L&D, QA, Recruitment, and frontline management — creating shared accountability across the entire new hire pipeline.
DMAIC SOP Redesign QA Integration Cross-functional Governance
Result
700 agents onboarded in 10 months
Attrition −20%
Nesting success rate 94%
Zero performance impact on live accounts.
The Challenge

Leadership committed to scaling the operation's two largest US accounts by 47% — from 1,500 to 2,200 agents — in under a year. The training function was already under pressure: attrition was climbing, performance metrics were declining, and the client had begun raising concerns. Hiring 700 more people through the same broken system would have amplified every existing problem.

The Approach

I rebuilt the foundations before scaling. First, I introduced qualitative in-class assessment activities that categorised agents by readiness during training itself — identifying at-risk individuals before they reached the floor and routing them into targeted support tracks rather than discovering the problem 30 days later during a performance review.

Second, I redesigned the full transition SOP from training to operations — the handover point where most attrition originates. Third, I established recurring cross-functional review calls connecting L&D, QA, Recruitment, and frontline and middle management, creating formal shared accountability for new hire performance for the first time.

The Result

700 agents were onboarded across 10 months with no measurable performance impact on live accounts. New hire attrition dropped 20%. Nesting success rate reached 94%. The training function transitioned from a point of client concern to a recognised strategic asset within the operation.

04 L&D Design Cutting the Learning Curve in Half
↓
What
New hire programmes were taking 6 weeks to reach minimum performance standards — inflating costs, creating operational backlogs during peak hiring, and delaying revenue. The programme hadn't been reviewed against actual performance data in over a year.
How
Ran a Kirkpatrick L1–L4 audit and found 40% of classroom time covered content agents never used in their first 30 days. Rebuilt the curriculum backwards from a 30-day performance map. Introduced gamification, Storyline simulations, and daily micro-coaching during nesting.
Storyline Kahoot Mentimeter Kirkpatrick L1–L4 Performance Mapping
Result
Speed to proficiency: 6 → 3.5 weeks (−42%)
Error rate −15%
Programme became cross-account standard.
The Challenge

A 6-week new hire programme had become the default across accounts — not because it was optimal, but because nobody had measured it against actual performance outcomes. The cost was significant: 6 weeks of salary before productive contribution, operational bottlenecks during high-volume hiring, and a backlog that put pressure on existing agents to absorb volume.

The Approach

I ran a Kirkpatrick Level 1–4 audit of the programme and found that 40% of classroom time was spent on content agents never accessed in their first 30 days on the floor. The curriculum had been built around what the business wanted to cover, not around what agents needed to perform.

I rebuilt it backwards from a 30-day performance map: what does a successful agent do in week one, week two, week three? Everything in the curriculum was traced to a specific performance output. I introduced Storyline-based simulations to replace passive content delivery, gamified critical knowledge retention through Kahoot and Mentimeter, and restructured nesting around daily micro-coaching touchpoints.

The Result

Speed to proficiency dropped from 6 to 3.5 weeks — a 42% reduction. Error rate fell 15%. The redesigned programme was adopted as the standard across all accounts. What had been a 6-week cost became a 3.5-week investment with measurably better outcomes at the other end.

05 L&D Transformation From Classroom to Digital at Scale
↓
What
All training was delivered face-to-face — expensive to scale, inconsistent across trainers, and leaving no reusable assets. As volume grew, training costs scaled linearly with headcount. I identified the opportunity and initiated the transformation before being asked to.
How
Built the business case, secured stakeholder and client buy-in, personally designed 70+ modules in Rise 360 and Articulate 360, deployed through SuccessFactors LMS with performance tracking. Upskilled the trainer team on blended digital delivery to maintain quality.
Rise 360 Articulate 360 SuccessFactors LMS Blended Learning Design
Result
Training costs −40%
70+ reusable digital modules built
Consistent delivery across all accounts
Scalable without proportional cost growth.
The Challenge

Every training session was delivered live by a trainer in a room. This meant every new hire needed a trainer's physical time, content quality varied by individual trainer, and nothing was reusable — every session started from scratch. As the operation scaled, training costs scaled proportionally. There was no leverage in the model.

Nobody had addressed this. I saw it as a structural problem with a structural solution.

The Approach

I initiated the transformation — built the business case, presented the ROI to operations leadership and secured client buy-in for the change in delivery model. I then personally designed over 70 modules in Rise 360 and Articulate 360: interactive branching scenarios, gamified product knowledge assessments, and self-paced foundational content that freed up live trainer time for complex skills that required human interaction.

Everything was deployed through SuccessFactors LMS with completion tracking and assessment scores linked to performance data. I also ran a train-the-trainer programme to ensure the team could facilitate blended delivery effectively rather than just playing videos.

The Result

Training costs reduced 40%. A library of 70+ reusable digital assets was built from zero. Delivery consistency improved measurably across all accounts — the content was the same regardless of which trainer facilitated the session. The operation could now scale headcount without scaling the training cost base proportionally.
